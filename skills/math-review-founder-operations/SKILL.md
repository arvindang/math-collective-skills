# MATH Review Founder Operations

Diagnose the operating system, not the founder's personality. Convert stated
strategy into evidence, constraints, accountable actions, and reviewable
decisions.

## Load the basis

Read [the source map](references/source-map.md) before giving source-backed
advice. Do not reproduce transcripts or carry historical anecdotes forward as
benchmarks. Use [the operating review](assets/founder-operating-review.md) as
the default output shape.

## Collect inputs

Request only missing inputs that can change the review:

- Company stage, business model, customer, jurisdiction, next company decision,
  review horizon, and founder's stated concern.
- Purpose, current strategy, product/customer/cash constraints, and initiative
  list with owners and deadlines.
- KPI definitions, current trends, recent cohorts or segments, targets, source
  systems, and known data-quality issues.
- Budget, actuals, cash, runway assumptions, hiring plan, and material
  commitments at an appropriate level of detail.
- Team structure, decision rights, leadership gaps, meeting cadence, and board
  or investor reporting.
- Promises made to customers, employees or contractors, and partners; delivery
  touchpoints; incentive systems; relevant policies; and known breaches.
- Immediate legal, safety, employment, privacy, security, solvency, or
  regulatory concerns.

Ask for redacted, aggregated material. Do not request names, protected traits,
individual compensation, health information, or sensitive personnel records
unless strictly necessary and authorized.

## Run the review

### 1. Define success and evidence

Name the decision this review must support. Set two to five observable outcomes,
a baseline, target, owner, evidence source, deadline, and next decision. Build
an evidence ledger that separates facts, interpretations, assumptions, and
unknowns. Preserve prior targets and commitments; do not rewrite history during
the review.

### 2. Find the binding constraint

Map the reinforcing loop among product value, customers, and cash. Add team,
trust, compliance, security, infrastructure, and other necessary constraints
rather than forcing every issue into revenue. Identify the one constraint that
most limits the next outcome and the evidence against that diagnosis.

Classify initiatives as must-do risk work, direct bottleneck relief,
fast-payback work, bounded experiment, or distraction. Assess cost, timing,
reversibility, dependencies, capacity, and time to evidence. Recommend what to
continue, change, pause, or stop.

### 3. Audit metrics and forecast integrity

Define entity, event, window, denominator, segment, source, owner, and cadence
for each material KPI. Show reconciled aggregates for accountability and recent
cohort, channel, segment, or marginal views for diagnosis. Test whether one
metric can improve by damaging another.

Compare plan with actuals. Trace variances to explicit drivers, volume, rate,
mix, timing, or data quality. Mark forecasts as conditional. Show cash low point,
runway range, confidence, and downside triggers; do not seed missing numbers
with source anecdotes.

### 4. Review execution and leadership scaling

Compare commitments with outcomes, owners, and decision latency. Identify
unclear authority, recurring handoff failure, missing capability, founder
bottlenecks, and meetings that transmit information without making decisions.
Define the next leadership behavior, delegation, or operating cadence required
by the company's current scale.

Review board and investor communication for interaction, stable definitions,
candid misses, material risks, explicit decisions, and useful asks. Treat
transparency as an operating control, not a performance ritual.

### 5. Operationalize stakeholder trust

For customers, employees or contractors, and partners, map the promised value
or experience, delivery touchpoint, responsible team, training and support,
measurement, compensation or incentive, data-custody duty, and breach signal.
Inspect whether short-term targets reward unsuitable selling, manipulative
engagement, weak frontline support, hidden bad news, or mishandled data.

Test whether leaders follow stated policies when an exception benefits them.
Record broken promises and resulting loyalty or business risk. Review
representation through decision authority, responsibility, economics, access,
and public credit—not headcount or optics alone.

Do not claim that trust causes superior shareholder returns. Treat the source's
historical workplace index, company stories, workforce statistics, platform
claims, portfolio results, and diversity percentages as illustrations requiring
current verification, not evidence-grade benchmarks.

### 6. Prioritize the operating reset

Recommend no more than three priorities for the review horizon. Give each an
owner, first action, evidence threshold, review date, dependency, and
continue/change/stop rule. Convert unresolved legal, accounting, tax,
employment, privacy, security, or governance questions into named professional
handoffs.

## Stop or ask

Stop the normal review and flag an urgent handoff when evidence suggests
payroll or solvency failure, fraud, harassment, worker or customer safety risk,
data breach, unlawful conduct, regulator contact, material fiduciary conflict,
or another situation needing qualified intervention.

Ask for direction when the decision, review horizon, company stage, or primary
evidence is missing; when requested actions would expose confidential data;
or when the user asks to contact stakeholders, alter systems, terminate or
classify workers, set compensation, approve financing, or make binding
governance decisions without explicit authority and review.

## Output contract

Deliver:

- Executive judgment, confidence, and information freshness date.
- Facts, assumptions, unknowns, and data-quality limits.
- Binding constraint and evidence against it.
- Operating scorecard across purpose, customer, execution, metrics, cash,
  leadership, governance, and trust.
- Initiative triage with continue/change/pause/stop decisions.
- KPI and forecast-integrity findings.
- Leadership and decision-rights changes.
- Stakeholder-promise and incentive conflicts.
- Top three priorities with owners, dates, and decision thresholds.
- Board agenda and urgent professional handoffs.
- Next-skill handoff with the operating-review artifact, missing inputs, and
  judgments the receiving skill should revisit rather than treat as facts.

End every output with **Watch/read next** and one to three task-relevant direct
source links selected from the source map. Put nothing after that section.

## Boundaries

- Verify laws, accounting definitions, market conditions, benchmark claims,
  and organization facts that may have changed. Date the review and label
  unverified claims.
- Coach observable behavior and system design. Never infer founder ability from
  demographic, personality, vacation, lifestyle, or other proxy traits.
- Minimize personnel and customer data. Avoid individual performance,
  compensation, classification, accommodation, investigation, or termination
  conclusions; route them to authorized HR and legal professionals.
- Do not give legal, tax, employment, securities, accounting, privacy,
  cybersecurity, or fiduciary conclusions.
- Use public-scrutiny as a pause test only; still test legality, consent,
  fairness, confidentiality, safety, and harm.
- Define capital efficiency relative to strategy, stakeholder obligations, and
  risk. Do not equate it with minimizing spend or shifting costs to workers,
  customers, or partners.
