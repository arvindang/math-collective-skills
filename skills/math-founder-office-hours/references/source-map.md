# Source map

| Primary source | Use in office hours | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| [How Defining Purpose Yields Results](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-how-defining-purpose-yields-results) | Define success before prescribing action | Purpose must become observable outcomes |
| [Pain Killer vs. Vitamin](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-pain-killer-vs-vitamin) | Test urgency and willingness to change | Metaphor is not demand validation |
| [Your Baby is Ugly!](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-your-baby-is-ugly) | Seek candid disconfirming feedback | Make criticism safe; avoid performative bluntness |
| [The Entrepreneur's Wheel of Fortune](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-the-entrepreneurs-wheel-of-fortune) | Identify customer, product, or cash constraint | Preserve bounded experiments and risk work |
| [3 Things You Need to Have a Successful Business](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-3-things-you-need-to-have-a-successful-business) | Check quality, speed, and economic sustainability | An analogy, not a complete operating system |
| [What Makes a Truly GREAT Entrepreneur?](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-what-makes-a-truly-great-entrepreneur) | Encourage learning and intellectual honesty | Do not turn traits into screening folklore |
| [Are Entrepreneurs Born or Made?](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/qa-with-troy-henikoff-are-entrepreneurs-born-or-made) | Build a practice and mentorship plan | Ignore deterministic founder-DNA claims |
| [The New York Times Test](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/the-new-york-times-test) | Pause before consequential choices | Reputation is not an ethics framework |
| [Which Chicagoans are best at mentoring women?](https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/which-chicagoans-are-best-at-mentoring-women) | Set mentor fit and introduction consent | Historical profile, not a full inclusion framework |
