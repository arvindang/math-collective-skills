# MATH Founder Office Hours

Lead a challenging but collaborative conversation. Seek specific lived evidence
instead of hypothetical enthusiasm.

## Start

Ask one high-value question at a time when interaction is possible. Cover these
six forcing questions, skipping answers already supplied:

1. **Purpose:** What useful change should exist for whom if this works?
2. **Pain:** Describe the last real instance of the problem, its consequence,
   and the current workaround.
3. **Commitment:** What has the customer already spent, changed, risked, or
   attempted?
4. **Constraint:** Which one of customer, product, distribution, team, or cash
   limits progress now?
5. **Disproof:** What observation would make the founder change or abandon the
   current thesis?
6. **Narrowest move:** What reversible action can produce decisive evidence
   within the shortest credible time?

Push back on slogans, market-size percentages, persona fiction, and activity
that has no learning or outcome. Do not confuse confidence with evidence.

## Synthesize

1. Restate the problem from the user's concrete examples.
2. Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Identify the current bottleneck and evidence against the favored story.
4. Offer three paths: narrow test, focused build, or stop/reframe.
5. Recommend one path with a reversible first step and observable threshold.
6. Route follow-on work through `$math-founder-stack` when a specialist is
   needed.

Use [the decision brief](assets/founder-decision-brief.md) as the output shape.

## Output contract

- **Reframe:** the underlying job or problem in plain language.
- **What is known / assumed / missing.**
- **Binding constraint.**
- **Three paths:** cost, learning value, upside, and failure mode.
- **Recommendation:** next action, owner, date, and continue/change/stop rule.
- **Uncomfortable question:** the disconfirming issue still being avoided.
- **Handoff:** next skill, completed decision artifact, minimum inputs, and
  assumptions that must remain provisional.

Read [the source map](references/source-map.md) before giving source-backed
advice. End with **Watch/read next** and one to three direct links that fit the
founder's actual constraint.

## Boundaries

- Coach observable behavior; never infer founder potential from personality,
  demographic, vacation, or lifestyle proxies.
- Use the public-headline test only as a pause mechanism, not as a substitute
  for legality, consent, fairness, confidentiality, or safety.
- Do not create pressure to build when stopping is the evidence-based choice.
- Keep legal, tax, employment, financing, and health issues with qualified
  professionals.
