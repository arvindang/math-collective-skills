# Structure founder equity

Prepare decisions and questions; do not practice law or tax. Make contributions, future commitments, governance, departure outcomes, and dilution visible before founders sign documents or issue securities.

## Load the source guide

Read [references/source-map.md](references/source-map.md) before analyzing. Apply every terminology and legal/freshness caution. Use the map to select final primary links. Do not quote or reproduce transcripts.

Use [assets/founder-equity-preflight.md](assets/founder-equity-preflight.md) as the default deliverable scaffold.

## Gather inputs

Collect or infer:

- Jurisdiction, entity type, formation date, tax residence, current date, and financing stage.
- Each founder's role, prior contribution, cash/IP contributed, opportunity cost, time commitment, decision rights, expected future work, and replacement difficulty.
- Current issued and authorized securities, purchase/issue dates, prices, vesting, repurchase rights, options/warrants, SAFEs/notes, promised grants, and unallocated pool.
- Signed governing, purchase, employment, IP-assignment, financing, and board/shareholder documents.
- Proposed vesting start, duration, cliff, acceleration, leave/departure treatment, repurchase, transfer, deadlock, and dispute terms.
- Hiring plan, compensation philosophy, cash constraints, grant instrument, current valuation/exercise price, and approval process.
- Planned financing amount, valuation definition, pool treatment, security terms, and possible future rounds.
- Decision deadline and dates that may trigger tax, securities, notice, or filing consequences.

Request the actual cap table and documents before calculating legal ownership. Redact personal identifiers and sensitive tax data where possible.

## Keep four evidence classes separate

Maintain these sections:

1. **Facts:** executed documents, issued securities, filed records, verified dates, and current cap-table entries.
2. **Assumptions:** proposed terms, future contributions, valuation, financing, departure, exit, and tax scenarios.
3. **Calculations:** arithmetic ownership, vesting schedules, dilution bridges, and scenario waterfalls, each tied to stated inputs.
4. **Judgments:** fairness views, governance tradeoffs, negotiation priorities, and professional-review recommendations.

Never treat a spreadsheet entry, handshake promise, or source example as proof of legal ownership. Never present “fair” as a mathematical fact.

## Run the preflight

1. **Frame the decision.** State whether the founders need an initial split, vesting design, departure scenario, compensation comparison, financing dilution view, or adviser question list.
2. **Build a founder matrix.** Record past contribution separately from future commitment. Include role criticality, time, cash/IP, risk, market compensation foregone, and decision responsibility without pretending these yield an objective percentage.
3. **Inventory governance.** Identify board/voting rights, reserved decisions, deadlock, information rights, IP ownership, confidentiality, conflicts, and dispute mechanisms.
4. **Map vesting and departures.** Show start date, cliff, periodic vesting, repurchase mechanics, acceleration, leave, disability/death, voluntary/involuntary departure, and termination scenarios. Flag missing or contradictory terms.
5. **Identify each instrument correctly.** Keep restricted stock, RSUs, ISOs, NSOs, warrants, SAFEs, and convertible notes distinct. Record purchase/exercise price, vesting, settlement, expiration, and approvals.
6. **Reconstruct capitalization.** List issued shares and all potentially dilutive securities. Define the fully diluted denominator and whether proposed pool increases occur pre- or post-money. Reconcile to governing documents.
7. **Model scenarios.** Show current ownership, proposed split, vesting over time, one or more founder departures, priced-round/pool dilution, and relevant conversion ranges. Label simple arithmetic separately from legal/economic rights.
8. **Evaluate total compensation.** Compare salary, benefits, equity type, fully diluted percentage, exercise/purchase cost, vesting, dilution, and scenario value. Do not add option count to salary dollars or imply guaranteed value.
9. **Create the adviser handoff.** List unresolved legal, tax, accounting, valuation, securities, employment, and governance questions with the governing document and deadline.
10. **Record the decision.** Capture agreed principles, open issues, owner, approval path, signing/filing dependencies, and next review date. Do not draft or file instruments unless the user separately requests authorized professional-document work.

## Apply issue-specific rules

### Initial founder split

Facilitate a discussion across contribution, future work, risk, control, and departure outcomes. Compare equal and unequal scenarios. Pair any split with explicit vesting/governance review. Do not call equal splits inherently wrong or prescribe a universal schedule.

### Restricted stock, RSUs, and possible 83(b) issues

Correct the source terminology: restricted stock is transferred property subject to restrictions; an RSU is generally a contractual promise to deliver value later. Do not assume an 83(b) election is available for an RSU. Treat eligibility, valuation, deadline, filing method, evidence of filing, and consequences as instrument-, taxpayer-, jurisdiction-, and date-specific. Route immediately to current U.S. tax and legal advice when Section 83(b) may matter.

### Employee or contractor equity

Treat cash and equity as a total package while keeping units economically distinct. For services-for-equity, surface scope, invoicing, IP, wage/classification, valuation, issuance, securities, tax, withholding/reporting, approval, and no-financing outcomes. Do not recommend circular payments as automatically tax-clean or cash-neutral.

### Financing and option pools

Define pre-money, post-money, fully diluted, pool top-up, and instrument versions. Show ownership and dilution, then list omitted rights such as preferences, participation, seniority, anti-dilution, pro rata, control, and conversion. Model several rounds when the decision depends on future capital.

## Stop or ask

- Ask for jurisdiction, entity type, instrument, issue/purchase date, and current documents when they affect the answer.
- Stop deadline-sensitive tax discussion and urge prompt qualified advice when a grant, transfer, exercise, or vesting event may already have occurred.
- Stop a definitive 83(b), option-tax, capital-gains, securities-exemption, valuation, or worker-classification conclusion.
- Stop precise cap-table, conversion, or waterfall calculations when signed documents, instrument versions, or fully diluted definitions are missing or inconsistent.
- Stop document drafting when the requested term could create legal rights and the user has not provided counsel-approved language or authorized a legal-document workflow.
- Ask before selecting founder percentages, vesting duration, cliff, acceleration, option-pool size, valuation, tax rate, or financing terms. Present scenarios rather than historical norms.
- Refuse backdating, concealing grants, misleading stakeholders, evading wage/tax/securities rules, or presenting an unapproved promise as issued equity.

## Professional-advice and freshness boundaries

Treat every output as educational issue-spotting and arithmetic, not legal, tax, accounting, valuation, investment, employment, fiduciary, or securities advice. Recommend qualified startup counsel and tax/accounting professionals before issuance, transfer, exercise, election, financing, termination, or material amendment.

Verify current official law, agency instructions, form versions, deadlines, tax rates, valuation requirements, and financing-document versions for the user's date and jurisdiction. State the as-of date and official source used. If current authoritative material is unavailable, stop at questions for counsel. Never rely on the source video's historical tax rates, mailing method, option-pool norm, or instrument terminology.

## Output contract

Deliver:

- Decision, jurisdiction, entity/stage, as-of date, and scope boundary.
- Separate facts, assumptions, calculations, and judgments.
- Founder contribution/commitment matrix and governance issue list.
- Instrument inventory with terminology and document provenance.
- Current/proposed fully diluted cap table only when supported by inputs.
- Vesting and departure scenarios plus financing/pool dilution where relevant.
- Fairness/tradeoff discussion without claiming a mathematically correct split.
- Deadline and risk flags, missing documents, and questions for counsel/tax/accounting advisers.
- Agreed next steps, approvals, owners, and review date.
- Handoff to professional review, modeling, or fundraising with the equity
  preflight artifact, governing-document gaps, and provisional scenarios.

End every user-facing deliverable with a heading exactly `Watch/read next` followed by 1–3 task-relevant direct primary-source links selected from [references/source-map.md](references/source-map.md). Link directly to lesson pages, not transcript copies or search results.
