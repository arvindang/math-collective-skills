# Source map

Use these sources for planning logic and cautions. Treat examples and heuristics as educational and dated unless independently refreshed.

| Source title | Direct URL | Use | Caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raise for Outcomes, Not Headlines | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-raise-for-outcomes-not-headlines | Work backward from next-round and exit outcomes instead of optimizing the current valuation | Reflects the 2021 market; a down round can be rational or necessary |
| Impact of Raising Too Much | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/impact-of-raising-too-much | Compare capital efficiency, dilution, and stakeholder outcomes across financing paths | Worked exit figures omit many waterfall, tax, and probability variables |
| When to Raise Capital | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-when-to-raise-capital | Size cash to a point of financing leverage and plan more than one move ahead | Near break-even is not the right milestone for every company |
| Funding the Valley | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/funding-the-valley | Stress-test enough runway to cross the difficult proof-building period | Full-ratchet prevalence and the example financing context are dated |
| The Secret of the Ask | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/secret-of-the-ask | Connect the amount raised to dated outcomes and distinguish the minimum from optional oversubscription | Momentum claims must be truthful; the minimum must still fund the plan |
| Back to Basics: Valuations, Option Pools and What You Need to Know Before Raising Capital | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-back-to-basics-valuations-option-pools-and-what-you-need-to-know-before-raising-capital | Reconstruct pre-money, post-money, option-pool, and fully diluted ownership | Pool treatment is negotiable and the example omits converts, warrants, and preferences |
| The Rule of Two | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-the-rule-of-two | Flag conversion overhang that could leave a priced round short of new operating cash | Two-to-one is a heuristic; SAFE and note mechanics differ |
| Convertible Debt/SAFE vs. Equity | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/math-101-convertible-debtsafe-vs-equity | Compare immediate simplicity with ownership, governance, and next-round clarity | The 2019 instrument framing predates newer forms and is a point of view |
| Higher Valuations Are Not Always Better | https://startups.henikoff.com/lesson/higher-valuations-are-not-always-better | Stress-test valuation against runway, milestones, and probability-weighted outcomes | Refresh comparables and analyze full terms; price alone is insufficient |
